# VTek Serial Booster 
*Paula* is overworked! She does a terrific job handling audio, interrupts and floppy,
but there just isn't time in her schedule to keep the serial port busy.

Enter the Serial Booster and *Buffy*, a companion for the DIP-48 *Paula* chip found in computers like the A500.
Buffy acts like a personal assistant to Paula, and she provides the deep FIFOs that ensure solid high speed serial communication without constant involvement by the CPU.
