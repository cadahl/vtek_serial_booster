# VTek Serial Booster 
*Paula* is overworked! She does a terrific job handling audio, interrupts and floppy,
but there *just isn't time* in her schedule to keep the serial port busy as well.

Enter the _VTek Serial Booster_ and *Buffy*, a companion for the DIP-48 *Paula* chip found in computers like the A500.
Buffy is like a personal assistant to Paula, providing (for the first time in history as far as I'm aware) her with deep FIFOs that ensure *solid high speed serial communication through the factory serial port* without constant involvement by the CPU.

The hardware itself, an interposer board placed between Paula and the motherboard, will be available for purchase at a very reasonable price. The software/firmware parts will be open source.

This is a work in progress, and more information is to come.
